People usually fall victim to factual information disguised as Life hacks but are in fact Myths. And thats where Clued comes in. A quiz game that helps users from being clueless to being CLUED IN!  

<h1>App Overview</h1>
People are exposed to lots of unverified life hacks and advice online, making it hard to tell what’s true and what’s a myth.

**Clued** solves this by using a quiz-based app where users test themselves with real-life scenarios and get instant feedback with explanations, helping them learn to distinguish between myths and genuine life hacks.

<h1> How to use the app</h1>
<li> Step 1: Select a quiz topic.</li>
<li> Step 2: Choose whether the quiz question is either a myth or life hack.</li>
<li> Step 3: Complete all quiz questions.</li>
 <li> Step 4: Click "Submit".</li>
<li> Step 5: View quiz results.</li>
<li> Step 6: Click on "Review Answers" to Review quiz answers supported by explanations. </li>
<li> Step 7:Click on "Back to home" to reselect a quiz topic.</li>
